Frequently Asked Questions

Does Ghost_FS require an internet connection?
No. Ghost_FileShuttler operates entirely within your local area network (LAN). As long as your devices (phones, laptops, tablets) are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or switch, file transfers happen at full hardware wire speed without touching the internet.
How does the PIN-based tenant isolation work?
Every unique PIN entered is hashed into a SHA-256 cryptographic digest. This digest acts as a tenant partition key in the database. Multiple producers on the same Wi-Fi entering different PINs will only see their own files.
How do I connect my iPhone or Android device?
Click the "QR Pair" button in the header and point your mobile camera at the on-screen QR code. Alternatively, type the displayed LAN IP into Chrome or Safari on your phone.
What file types and sizes are supported?
All file formats are supported: uncompressed audio stems (WAV, FLAC, AIFF), MP3s, videos, PDFs, and ZIP archives up to 10 GB per partition. Audio files automatically feature in-browser waveform playback!
